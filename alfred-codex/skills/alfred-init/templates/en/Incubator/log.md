# Incubator — where questions and half-ideas land

## Goal

Keep what doesn't belong to any current project: questions asked out of curiosity, things studied, stray research thoughts,
doubts that come up while working on something else. Where they came from doesn't matter.

## Premises

- Incubator is itself a project (code INC) and also the home of sub-projects that aren't launched elsewhere yet (common rules, section 2)
- A sub-project is launched only when I say so: `Incubator/<sub>/` with its own code · log · trace, status incubating.
  Its workspace is `project-workspace/Incubator/<sub>/` with its own git
- When a sub-project grows, its whole folder (log side and workspace side) moves to the category where it will continue.
  If I drop it, it goes to Archive
- No decision.md here — Incubator has no single purpose. A sub-project's "why" waits in its open item until launch (common rules, section 14)
